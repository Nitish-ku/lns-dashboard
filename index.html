How it's counted

Booking and tier

Every appointment ROAS puts on an AI Launch Call calendar in GHL since Oct 11, 13:56 UTC. Tier is the calendar it landed on: A, B or C. The few booked straight onto a closer's own AI Launch calendar count in "All" only.

Confirmed

The lead said yes to attending: Close "Confirmed Booking" is Yes, LNS marked it CONFIRMED, or the lead texted a clear yes before the call. Cancelled bookings are left out of the confirm rate.

Showed

Set by the closers' EOC form through the Close opportunity: follow-up, deposit, won, lost or disqualified means the call happened; "No Show" means it didn't. Show rate = showed ÷ (showed + no-show).
