07

How it's counted

Applications and booked calls

An application is a completed ROAS form. A booked call is an AI Launch Call on the GHL calendar whose time has passed; calls still to come are left out until they happen. A call the lead cancelled through the ROAS link is left out too; a call the team cancelled counts as a no-show.

Confirmed

The lead said yes to attending: Close "Confirmed Booking" is Yes, LNS marked it CONFIRMED, or the lead texted a clear yes before the call.

Booking types

Direct: ROAS booked a Tier A or B lead straight onto a closer calendar. Setter-led: a setter booked a Tier C or unbooked lead onto a closer calendar (booked by a person rather than ROAS, after a triage call, or a setter form saying "Closer Booking"). Recovery: a closer call booked again after the lead no-showed or the team cancelled. Follow-up: a later call after one that showed, or any call on Aman's or Nabeel's follow-up link. The setter's Booking Type on the triage form overrides all of this.

Shows

A show is an EOC form saying the call happened: the Closer EOC (Tier A and B) or the Setter triage EOC (Tier C), read from Airtable. Anything else is a no-show, including a form left blank. Show rate = shows ÷ booked calls. The show rate covers closer calls (Tier A and B); Tier C triage calls have their own rate in section 02. A call the closer marks "client cancelled" is left out, like a cancellation through the ROAS link.
